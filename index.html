// how it works

From a million lines to one answer.

  1. 01

    Parse

    Streaming parser auto-detects the format and mines each line into a template.

  2. 02

    Embed & cluster

    One vector per unique template, TF-IDF or MiniLM, clustered by meaning.

  3. 03

    Detect & group

    An ensemble scores every template; repeats collapse into ×N incident families.

  4. 04

    Explain

    Every anomaly ships with a plain-English reason. Add --rca for an AI root-cause story.
