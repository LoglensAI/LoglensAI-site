What's inside

Everything you need to catch
the incident before your users do

One install. No account, no agent, no cloud.

Turbo & deep engines

Statistical turbo mode scans ~52k lines/sec; optional neural deep mode adds transformer embeddings for the highest precision.

100% private

Detection runs entirely on your box. Logs never leave your machine - GDPR / HIPAA / air-gap friendly by design.

Explains itself

Every anomaly ships with a plain-English reason - not just a number. Know why a line was flagged, instantly.

AI root-cause

Bring your own key (OpenAI / Azure / Groq) for a written root-cause narrative. Only grouped summaries are sent - never raw logs.

Incident grouping

200 identical errors collapse into one incident family with an ×N count. Signal, not noise.

10+ formats, auto-detected

Apache, Linux, HDFS, Spark, Zookeeper, OpenStack, BGL and more - zero config. Reads files, stdin, URLs, or live commands.

How it works

From raw logs to ranked incidents

Parse

Auto-detects the format and normalizes every line into a typed record.

Embed & cluster

Learns recurring templates and represents them with statistical or neural embeddings.

Detect & group

Flags outliers, scores severity, and collapses repeats into incident families.

Explain

Attaches a plain-English reason - and an optional AI root-cause narrative.

Fits how you already work

Drop it in, in one line

python - self-alerting
import logging
from loglens import LogLensHandler

# your app now alarms on its own
logging.getLogger().addHandler(
    LogLensHandler(on_anomaly=alert_me)
)
